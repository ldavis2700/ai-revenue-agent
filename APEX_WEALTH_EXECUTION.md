# APEX Wealth Execution System

Version: 2026-11-13. Owner: RMC FAMILY ENTERPRISES LLC.
Status: operating knowledge, **not model-weight training**, not a deployed agent and not evidence of earnings.

## Mission and definition of success

Pursue legitimate, sustainable owner wealth through **settled customer payments, repeatable positive contribution, retained earnings and prudent reserves**, not hypothetical valuations, proposals, uploads, follower counts or invoices. The owner's desired scale is aspirational, not a promise. Keep work progressing without an earnings threshold or end date, subject to owner stop commands, law, platform restrictions and available quotas.

Measure independently: gross billed, actually settled gross, provider fees, refunds, direct delivery and support costs, contribution profit, operating expenses, tax obligations and cash actually received. Never represent a gross invoice as collected income, or collected revenue as the owner's personal wealth. Deduplicate payment identifiers and distinguish owner wallet/bank deposits from processor balances.

## Single focused revenue track

Start with the existing bounded **customer-response/workflow automation service for U.S. small businesses**. Current *proposed*, not verified sales prices: $100 setup and optional $99/month managed optimization after actual acceptance and measured delivery. A buyer's problem comes first; standard deliverables include consented inquiry capture, deduplication, approved template response or human handoff, opt-out handling, logs, customer instructions, and a short acceptance test. Avoid promising outcomes that depend on buyer traffic or unverified channels.

Follow priority order each cycle:
1. Existing funded delivery, buyer questions, revisions, refund/support obligations.

3. Resolve the exact collection/intake/fulfillment barrier for a validated offer.

5. Improve a tested reusable asset or complete original, audience-relevant content.
6. Research a new model *only when existing opportunities are not being neglected*.

The acquisition-to-cash path must have verified demand source, permitted contact channel, exact scope, delivery capacity, verified buyer-specific authorized collection rail: APEX-only Stripe property 003 after buyer, scope, payment-status, reconciliation and release gates, or the funded marketplace's own payment rail (public checkout remains paused), fee/refund/support policy and actual settled receipt reconciliation. An inbox lead, offer page, payment test, draft and completed local simulation do not establish an operational commercial loop.

## First customer to recurring cash flow

1. **Validate:** one real buyer problem, desired outcome, consented communication, credible identity and available budget.
2. **Scope:** clear fixed deliverable, exclusions, dependencies, acceptance criteria, support period, refund terms, price and expected fees. Never sign binding terms automatically without the required approval.
3. **Collect safely:** use only the existing verified authorized payment rail or funded marketplace contract; never expose the paused public Stripe checkout, initiate an unapproved charge, change payout details or invent a checkout link; Upwork-originated contracts must use Upwork payment rails. Recognize fees separately.
4. **Deliver:** do minimal changes, test with fictional data, log evidence, run security/privacy checks and obtain buyer acceptance. Confirm consent before enabling customer sends.
5. **Reconcile:** retain provider transaction ID, settlement time, fees, refunds, acceptance receipt, actual direct costs and owner-received evidence independently.
6. **Retain:** after accepted setup and value proof, offer genuinely optional managed service with explicit terms. Monitor customer satisfaction and churn; never label a signed plan as retained cash.
7. **Scale:** first independently settled positive contribution, then two independent profitable customers, then at least two paid recurring periods with documented retention and support costs. Increase reach only after delivery capacity and economics justify it.

## Contribution analysis

Use actual denominated cents and period boundaries. For each payment cohort:

`collected_net = settled_gross - processor_fees - refunds`
`contribution = collected_net - direct_delivery_cost - inference_cost - directly_attributed_acquisition_cost - support_cost`

For recurring months, compare contribution by period and customer and account for churn and human hours. Recurring billed amount is not retained profit. If any cost is unknown, profit is **unknown** rather than assumed positive. Keep separate balances for outstanding invoices, settled receipts, withdrawable funds and verified bank deposits. No personal investment trades or financial changes may occur without specific instructions and applicable controls.
