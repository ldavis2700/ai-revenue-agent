# Weekly AI business-model to verified cash execution

**Owner directive: November 10, 2026.** Every week APEX should identify successful legitimate AI-executable business models, adapt their public business mechanisms into original offers, and pursue real customer cash flow. This is an execution protocol, not a claim that any model has been copied, sold, fulfilled, or paid.

Use the existing Monday 9 AM Pacific APEX SMB brief and existing hourly APEX Wealth Engine; do not create duplicate recurrent workflows. The existing Business Model Intelligence GitHub Action performs weekly deterministic ranking and tests. It does **not** browse current markets or execute buyers' work. The Monday connected workflow must gather dated live buyer evidence and pass genuine facts to `scripts/weekly_model_to_cash.py --evidence <private-observations.json>` (keep private buyer details out of GitHub artifacts). Store concise evidence and the next feasible conversion step in the existing durable APEX revenue checkpoint.

## What “most successful” means

Never assert an absolute global champion without complete data. Compare currently observable market demand, verified paid buyer intent, delivery feasibility, legally usable account/channel, gross versus actual contribution, startup/ongoing support costs, speed to collected cash, repeatability, customer retention, competition, platform/compliance requirements, and APEX-specific demonstrated capability. Cite current official/public sources and separately record private consented buyer evidence without leaking it. Label marketing earnings claims, projections and rankings as such, not collected revenue.

## Workflow each Monday

1. Inspect all current funded jobs, real buyer messages, revisions, refunds and available settlement evidence FIRST. Fulfill accepted paid work before researching novel models.
2. Compare up to ten candidates from `config/business_models*.json` and current real opportunity evidence, including customer-response automation, CRM integration, narrow software repair/QA, and tested AI workflows. Read the existing portfolio ranking as priors only.
3. Select at most two unvalidated opportunities. Record exact buyer pain, provenance, deliverable, effort, fees, allowable channel, permissions, realistic cost and optional recurring support. Do not invent access, margins, buyer enthusiasm or provider receipts.
4. Prepare a distinct lawful offer/pilot, original QA-tested delivery asset, consent-based intake and truthful tailored proposal. **Recreate a business mechanism, not brand identity, websites, copyrighted assets, proprietary code, private data or unlicensed content.**
5. Before any revenue action validate the path `qualified demand → consented communication → accepted scope/funding → QA and buyer acceptance → authorized non-Stripe settlement → fees/refunds/costs → net contribution`. Stripe stays paused. No unapproved Connects, API usage, ads, recurring memberships, paid models, binding contracts, cold spam, account charges or bank/payout changes.
6. Execute independently authorized reversible free actions. For provider-required exact approvals, preserve specific draft and blocked operation; never mark it submitted/accepted without a receipt.
7. Reconcile all cash from independently settled provider transactions. Separate draft, proposal submitted, buyer reply, funded contract, accepted delivery, invoice, settlement, processor balance, bank receipt and owner profit. Unknown costs mean unknown profit.

## Success thresholds

First genuine settled paid delivery; two independent customers with positive evidenced contribution; at least two retained paid recurring periods with costs; then scale only after clear deliverability and retention. Do not stop because of zero income. The existing hourly task continues queue/fulfillment/content and revenue work independent of Monday research.

## Safety and tooling

`scripts/weekly_model_to_cash.py` is a **fail-closed preparation gate** requiring supplied factual fields, sources and permission. Its input is never automatically verified against provider billing or private buyer channels; operators must independently authenticate it. `prepare_only` means only permission to prepare drafts and QA assets, not permission to contact buyers, deploy, invoice, sign or collect. Its estimate is not profit. Keep company credentials and personal buyer data outside public GitHub. The weekly Action outputs only the existing non-sensitive catalog candidate snapshot; buyer receipts and actual economics belong in a protected ledger. Coordinate technical repairs with Hourly App Completion.

The next concrete conversion step while no funded buyer is verified is to establish a permitted non-Stripe payment route for the existing $100 consent-first customer-response setup, then complete one genuine requested pilot with QA, accepted deliverable and provider receipt. Avoid spawning a speculative app to satisfy the weekly report.

## Original recreation and monetization readiness (November 10, 2026)

`scripts/weekly_model_to_cash.py` also returns a separate `recreation` gate for each candidate. To claim that an externally inspired business mechanism has been recreated for a real buyer, independently verify original licensed assets (never imitate another company's identity or copy private/proprietary materials), an immutable SHA-256 of the tested delivery, HTTPS QA evidence, and a real buyer acceptance record. Missing evidence leaves the work in `preparation_only`. Complete operator-supplied fields yield `acceptance_evidence_supplied`, **not** proven fulfillment or cash: the script cannot independently verify claims, issue contracts, publish, charge or inspect buyer accounts. Real provider delivery and settlement receipts remain separately mandatory; statuses never grant permission to bypass reviews.

Do not attempt to reproduce all popular business models. Prioritize funded requests and the existing narrow U.S. SMB customer-response offer; grow recurring support only after settled payments, proven direct costs and buyer retention. Continue the existing Monday research and hourly execution tasks even with no earnings, but do not call one background schedule a continuously running 24/7 worker.

## Continuous learning and current business-model expertise

The owner's November 10 directive makes learning, keeping current, and executing
successful AI-enabled business models an indefinite objective. Maintain broad
coverage in the opportunity backlog, while executing a bounded number of useful
experiments. Expertise must develop through tested delivery and repeated real
customer outcomes; reading, rankings and self-reported knowledge do not prove it.

Use the existing Monday brief for comparative discovery, rotating through the
catalog so other viable models are not permanently ignored. Every existing
hourly run resumes funded delivery first, checks material changes affecting
active models, and advances the next feasible research, skill test, delivery or
collection step. Persist the playbook and actual outcome in existing private
records, including failures. Do not create a second scheduler or reset ledgers.

Each candidate can carry `learning_evidence` keyed by these topics:

| Topic | Default review interval | Required practical knowledge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `customer_demand` | 7 days | Buyer problem, dated demand, competing solutions |
| `pricing_and_fees` | 7 days | Observed prices, fees, scope and pricing assumptions |
| `platform_rules_and_rights` | 7 days | Eligibility, quotas, commercial rights, obligations |
| `acquisition_and_consent` | 7 days | Available permitted channel, intake and conversion steps |
| `delivery_and_qa` | 30 days | Original delivery procedure, skill test and acceptance criteria |
| `collection_and_unit_economics` | 7 days | Authorized payment path, costs, refunds and settlement evidence |
| `retention_and_failure_lessons` | 30 days | Repeat value, measured outcomes, failed assumptions and next test |

Each topic contains `finding`, `next_action`, `source_urls` (HTTPS), and its own
timezone-aware `checked_at`. Set `material_change: true` when a change invalidates
the finding; clear it only after actually reviewing and updating the evidence.
Use current authoritative sources and genuine permitted buyer/provider records.
These intervals are operating defaults; known changes require immediate review.
Never refresh a date without checking the source and updating the practical lesson.

`scripts/weekly_model_to_cash.py --evidence <private-observations.json>` now returns
a per-candidate `learning` review and `learning_refresh_queue`, even for candidates
missing buyer evidence. Missing, malformed, future-dated or stale topic records
produce targeted refresh tasks. The queue follows input order, not a claim of
global market ranking. Funded obligations still take precedence. A current brief
does not override the separate preparation, permission or recreation gates.

The checker validates metadata only: it does not browse, verify source contents,
train model weights, perform skill tests, declare expertise or execute an external
action. The connected hourly/Monday workflows gather evidence and carry out
authorized work; this change does not deploy the standalone revenue worker.
Treat supplied evidence as unverified until independently checked. Keep source
findings and buyer details private; the learning summary emits topic statuses
and refresh dates without echoing findings or source credentials.

For each selected viable model, proceed from studied requirements to original
tested pilot, buyer acceptance, settled payment and repeated positive contribution.
Record the exact next action and blocker. Scale on demonstrated delivery and
economics; revise or retire failing experiments while continuing the overall
mission. No end date or revenue milestone ends the objective. Owner controls,
provider permissions and actual runtime availability remain authoritative.

## Evidence-to-execution selection (November 10, 2026 Pacific)

The preparation shortlist now requires all seven learning topics to be documented
and current, the existing buyer/access/collection gates, and a bounded
`execution_plan`. A high projected margin alone cannot occupy an experiment slot.
Missing information remains in `learning_refresh_queue` and
`execution_preparation_queue`; it is not silently discarded. This does not defer
existing funded fulfillment while a research checklist is incomplete.

Each private candidate's `execution_plan` contains:

| Fields | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `hypothesis`, `customer_segment` | Falsifiable buyer problem and specific audience |
| `acceptance_test`, `measurement_method` | Delivery checks and how independent outcomes will be counted |
| `success_metric` | `qualified_opt_in_requests`, `buyer_accepted_deliveries`, or `settled_paid_deliveries` |
| `baseline`, `target` | Nonnegative integer counts in comparable measurement windows; target exceeds baseline |
| `minimum_sample_size` | Minimum independent observations before interpreting the result |
| `stop_rule`, `review_at` | Explicit revise/retire criterion and timezone-aware review deadline within 14 days |
| `next_action` | One concrete feasible action, with evidence of completion recorded afterward |
| `estimated_total_minutes`, `estimated_days_to_cash` | All acquisition, delivery, QA and support effort; realistic time to collection |
| `success_probability_bps` | Conservative estimated chance of the forecast revenue, in basis points from 0 to 10000 |
| `probability_basis`, `probability_evidence_url` | Rationale and HTTPS provenance; unknown probabilities must stay unknown, not invented to pass |
| `max_new_spend_cents` | Exactly zero under this preparation policy; existing approved costs still belong in the economics |

The ranking uses estimated expected contribution per total work hour, then shorter
time to cash, with model ID as a stable tie-breaker. Expected contribution is
`estimated revenue × probability − all estimated costs`. Costs are charged even
when the sale fails. Costs must include tools, fees, refunds/rework allowances and
support; do not double-count labor already represented in cash costs. A nonpositive
expected contribution remains a research/revision task. These are conservative
planning estimates, not verified income, a statistical confidence interval or
proof of the world's most profitable business. Probability sensitivity must be
reviewed against contradictory evidence before committing resources.

At most two new models are shortlisted per report. Reconcile the existing active
experiment ledger before starting anything: the two-model limit applies across
runs, not two additional experiments every hour. Duplicate model IDs and batches
larger than 30 are rejected. Reported queues omit private hypotheses, URLs and
buyer details; use the protected original evidence record to execute the step.

During the existing hourly cycle, fulfill funded work first, then refresh the
specific expired/changed facts, complete original delivery QA, and advance the
next authorized experiment action. At the deadline, compare the measured result
with its baseline, target, sample size and stop rule. Insufficient observations
mean inconclusive, not success. Persist failed assumptions, acceptance evidence,
refunds, settled receipts and actual costs. Feed measured outcomes through the
existing experiment observation/learning and verified revenue ledgers without
converting views, forecasts or self-reported evidence into sales.

Scale only after independently verified accepted deliveries and repeat positive
contribution; verify retention before promising recurring economics. Revise or
retire an unsuccessful experiment while continuing other authorized work. All
code outputs remain preparation-only and source-unverified: no field grants
permission to send, publish, charge, sign, spend or deploy. This changes the
selection tool used by connected workflows, not the deployed content server or
standalone revenue worker, and does not train a model's weights.
