## Practical business mechanism

Locate residential properties that may have motivated, **legally authorized** sellers; evaluate defensible after-repair/resale value and buyer demand; after professional legal review and the required owner approvals, seek an assignable purchase agreement or approved closing structure; qualify a buyer; close through a lawful title/escrow provider. Fees count as APEX revenue only when actually settled, attributed, and reconciled.

Neither inherited status nor absentee ownership alone indicates motivation, valid authority to sell, financial difficulty, or consent to be contacted. Do not exploit bereavement, infer death from unverified signals, or use invasive people searches.

## Daily, bounded autonomous research cycle

1. **Check gates first:** record the state/county, dated official legal sources, licensing and equitable-interest marketing requirements, assignment rules, probate executor/representative authority, title/escrow rules, fair housing, privacy, DNC/TCPA/CAN-SPAM. If unresolved, mark jurisdiction `blocked` and perform no seller/buyer outreach or offer activity.
2. **Collect permitted data:** only authorized public-record/data-provider feeds with documented rate/terms, at zero marginal cost by default. Track source URL/date, parcel ID, record owner and *verified* sale authority where available, legal description, assessor record, listing status, occupancy uncertainty, mortgage/equity uncertainty, taxes, liens, title flags, condition/repairs, recent **closed** comparable sales and buyer demand evidence. Never scrape behind access controls or purchase a paid subscription without approval.
3. **Validate and deduplicate:** block properties with uncertain estate authority, unverifiable owner identity, title defects, stale data, unsupported equity numbers, impossible price spreads, or insufficient comp evidence. Never auto-contact bereaved relatives.
4. **Underwrite conservatively:** calculate buyer-supported sale range, repair/holding expense, title/legal/inspection and closing costs, reserve for unknowns, buyer margin and potential *disclosed* assignment fee. Label all estimates with uncertainty and stop if conservative net is nonpositive.
5. **Research buyer demand:** consented investor lists and legitimate public buyer criteria only; distinguish a potential buyer from verified proof of funds and from completed purchases.
6. **Prepare nonbinding packets:** one sourced evidence packet per qualified lead with suitability score, caveats, compliance status, permitted next action and draft terms **for review**. Do not advertise a property or contractual interest that APEX lacks legal rights to market.
7. **Record the run:** append an idempotent date/model/jurisdiction-keyed result in the **existing** APEX model evidence system: sources, leads reviewed, rejected and why, qualified research packets, blockers, research cost/time, approvals pending and next safe action. Revenue defaults to $0 until independent settlement confirmation. Prioritize verified paid obligations over speculative leads; stop expensive/low-yield channels.

## Hard human, legal, and financial gates

No automatic outreach without jurisdiction-specific legal/consent review; no automated unsolicited phone/text/email campaigns; no seller exploitation or false ownership/buyer claims. Contracts, binding offers, signatures, escrow, earnest money, paid services, property acquisition, assignment, marketing an equitable interest, and closing require applicable licenses, lawful authority, suitable professional review, and deal-level approvals. APEX cannot substitute its own legal opinion.

## Acceptance checks before claiming 'runs daily'

- Code is integrated with existing APEX model portfolio runner with **idempotent** daily scheduling (no independent duplicate job).
- Unit tests: uncertain probate authority, purported inheritance without evidence, false equity, missing reliable closed comps, missing assignment/marketing rights, jurisdiction blocked, duplicates, unlawful outreach, negative net margin, and unverified paid fees.
- Demonstrate a successful dry run using synthetic leads and a source-linked lawful market research sample; show scheduling and logs from deployed worker.
- If deployment is pending or sources unavailable, report accurately **configured but not operating**, not autonomous earnings.

## Scale criteria

Promote only when jurisdiction gates are satisfied, reliable lawful inputs exist, buyers and transactions have independently verified evidence, and net settled revenue exceeds all variable and human operating costs. Compare opportunity cost against APEX's better-validated service clients. Do not buy access to the advertised platform without independent cost/benefit evidence.
