# Owned training → tailored setup → optional managed support

Owner direction, November 10, 2026 (Pacific): adopt the training/product/service business model without subscribing to Kajabi. The $40,000/month objective is a gross-revenue target, not a forecast or guarantee.

## Implemented first offer

APEX Response Lab is an original, free four-lesson course for residential cleaning businesses at `/academy`, on the existing APEX acquisition site. It includes a browser-only response-template builder, a downloadable one-week measurement worksheet, and a campaign-tagged link to the existing consented preview request. It adds no vendor, AI API, tracking SDK, account requirement, subscription, or checkout dependency. Existing hosting costs and quotas still apply.

The narrow niche is a test hypothesis. No buyer validation, collected revenue, or conversion improvement is asserted. The course is public lead-generation material, not a protected paid product. Progress is stored as lesson identifiers in the visitor's browser; business-name inputs are not persisted or transmitted by the builder.

The existing $100 response-asset setup and optional $99/month optimization remain the initial service offers. They are not a live chatbot installation or automatic customer messaging. An inquiry is not a sale or permission to subscribe someone. The current checkout pause remains in force.

## Business mechanism

1. Attract relevant visitors with original demonstrations and practical lessons through authorized owned channels.
2. Let visitors use a useful free sample without surrendering an email address.
3. Offer a separate, affirmative request for a tailored preview; record academy campaign attribution through existing intake.
4. Confirm buyer needs, delivery scope, support/refund terms, and an authorized payment route before accepting a paid engagement.
5. Deliver the scoped response assets, test them against business facts and escalation cases, obtain acceptance, and reconcile settlement and costs.
6. Offer recurring support only when the customer has a continuing need and affirmatively accepts its scope and recurring terms.
7. Develop a separately priced, protected training product only after repeated positive-margin delivery reveals reusable content worth buying.

Use the existing `online_course`, `digital_templates`, `productized_service`, and `managed_service` catalog entries. Do not invent a new globally “best” business model, promote mastery, or mark delivery/payment capability verified because this page exists. Continue funded obligations ahead of new speculative course development.

## Validation experiment

First milestone: three genuine customers with settled payments and accepted scoped deliveries, followed by documented delivery economics. Start with up to ten voluntary buyer discovery conversations through permitted channels; this number is a research target, not a mailing instruction. Do not fabricate urgency or client results.

Track qualified academy-attributed inquiries, requested previews, scoped proposals, settled purchases, refunds, delivery hours, and optional renewals. Traffic and conversion percentages remain unknown until a reliable denominator exists. A downloaded worksheet is not a customer. Record private buyer evidence in protected systems, never in this public repository.

After ten qualified conversations, revise the niche or offer if no one identifies a paid problem. After three paid deliveries, compare price with actual labor, acquisition, support, processor fees, and refunds. Scale only when contribution remains positive. These are operating decision rules, not market statistics.

## Path toward $40,000/month

Do not extrapolate the initial $100 setup into a validated growth plan. At today's prices, 100 new setups plus 304 active $99 subscriptions would total $40,096/month gross, before all costs; serving and acquiring that volume is unproven and may be impractical.

A later illustrative mix could be 40 training purchases at $500 ($20,000), five separately scoped implementations at $3,000 ($15,000), and ten support accounts at $500 ($5,000): $40,000 gross. Those prices, demand, higher-scope delivery capability, and capacity are unvalidated hypotheses, not current offers or permission to change billing. The $35,000 from new purchases and implementations would have to be earned again each month; only $5,000 in that illustration is recurring. No target date is supported yet.
