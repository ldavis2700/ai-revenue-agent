## Official source and policy review (checked November 13, 2026)

- [Fannie Mae HomePath](https://homepath.fanniemae.com/homepath-faq): First Look is **30 days** for eligible owner occupants and community entities; agents submit offers. Fannie Mae cautions that hazards may be incompletely known and recommends inspection. The [property finder](https://homepath.fanniemae.com/property-finder) does not verify eligibility for a particular asset.
- [Freddie Mac HomeSteps](https://www.homesteps.com/homebuyer/firstlook): a **30-day** First Look period favors owner occupants and specified organizations. Investors may be considered afterward, but the listing broker/MLS and any HOA restrictions control individual eligibility. Never falsify owner-occupancy affidavits.
- [HUD FHA notice published April 28, 2025](https://www.hud.gov/hud-partners/single-family-fha-info): revised the HUD exclusive REO period from 30 to **15 days** for insured/insured-with-escrow homes. Mortgagee Letter 2025-13 prescribes **five days for uninsured** homes; [HUD Handbook 4000.1](https://www.hud.gov/hud-partners/single-family-fha-info) reflects the policy. [HUD's HUD Homes guide](https://www.hud.gov/helping-americans/hudhomes-how-to-sell) says HUD-registered brokers submit purchase contracts. No active HUD inventory was verified in this sample.
- [Georgia Real Estate Commission rules](https://rules.sos.ga.gov/gac/520-1.In): unlicensed support staff face restrictions on brokerage activities, commissions, listed-property advice and negotiations. APEX has **no verified brokerage license or counsel-reviewed state-specific service scope**. Original research only; no transaction-based fee or broker representation.

## Reproducible offline evaluation

Run the offline source processor using the following shell commands:

    python scripts/reo_source_screen.py --input docs/research/reo_sample_2026-11-13.json
    python -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_reo_source_screen.py' -v

It never accesses the network, contacts anyone, charges, posts, bids or writes to a customer database. A 90-day trigger requires a dated initial listing; two cuts require ordered dated sourced prices. Missing and conflicting inputs fail closed. Three closed comparable sales with URLs, cited ARV, a **local sourced repair low/high**, and itemized acquisition, resale, holding and uncertainty costs are prerequisites for the illustrative 70%-of-ARV scenario:

**Screening maximum = max(0, 0.70 × sourced ARV – high repairs – acquisition – resale – holding – uncertainty reserve).**

This is **not** an appraisal, offer recommendation or guaranteed seller concession. Inspection, ownership/title, liens, occupancy, financing, restrictions, tax consequences and legal scope remain unverified.
