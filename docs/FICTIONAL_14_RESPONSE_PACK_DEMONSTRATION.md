**This is an original, illustrative practice example, not real buyer work.** “Example Cleaning Co.”, “Example County”, its services and processes are invented placeholders for explaining the format. No customer, quote, booking, review, sale, result, payment, delivery, or acceptance is being represented. Do not attribute these words to a real company or send them without checking real facts and permission.

## Example-only business profile

- **Business name:** Example Cleaning Co. — fictional
- **Hypothetical services:** routine residential cleaning and move-out cleaning
- **Hypothetical area:** Example County, USA — placeholder, not a verified location
- **Example quote process:** owner reviews scope, approximate size and special tasks; no price is promised automatically
- **Escalation:** fictional owner for any price, availability, complaint, access, insurance, or policy question

## Fourteen draft replies (seven situations, two options each)

### 1. New customer inquiry

**Draft 1 — option 1.** Thanks for contacting Example Cleaning Co. What kind of cleaning are you considering, and what general area is the space in? A person will confirm whether we can help before any booking.

**Draft 2 — option 2.** Welcome to Example Cleaning Co. Is your question about a one-time cleaning or recurring visits? If you share the general scope and preferred timeframe, the owner can review the next step.

### 2. Price and quote request

**Draft 3 — option 1.** Thanks for requesting an estimate. Our example process is to review the type of cleaning, approximate size, general area and any special tasks before an owner confirms a quote. Please do not send payment or access details here.

**Draft 4 — option 2.** To help the owner understand your quote request, is this a routine home cleaning or a move-out cleaning, and are there any special areas you want reviewed? No price or availability is confirmed by this message.

### 3. Missed call response

**Draft 5 — option 1.** Sorry we missed your call to Example Cleaning Co. If you still need help, which cleaning service were you calling about? A team member will review your request.

**Draft 6 — option 2.** Thanks for calling. What general time window is convenient for an owner-reviewed response? Please avoid including a door code, payment details or other sensitive information.

### 4. Permitted follow-up to an existing inquiry

**Draft 7 — option 1.** Following up once on the cleaning request you sent to Example Cleaning Co. Is there a detail you would like us to clarify? If you do not want further follow-up, please let us know.

**Draft 8 — option 2.** You previously requested details about cleaning services. Are you still considering the service, or would you prefer to close the inquiry? No appointment is booked by this message.

### 5. After-service review request

**Draft 9 — option 1.** Thank you for giving Example Cleaning Co. the opportunity to complete your service. If you wish, you may share an honest review of your experience, including anything we could improve. Feedback is optional.

**Draft 10 — option 2.** If you would like to tell us how the cleaning went, your candid feedback is welcome. We do not offer a reward for any rating or ask only satisfied customers to respond.

### 6. Returning customer inquiry

**Draft 11 — option 1.** Welcome back to Example Cleaning Co. Are you looking for the same type of cleaning or something different this time? An owner will confirm scope, availability and price before scheduling.

**Draft 12 — option 2.** Thanks for reaching out again. Has the general cleaning area or requested service changed since your last appointment? Please do not send access codes; the owner can check your request privately.

### 7. Common questions and escalation

**Draft 13 — option 1.** The example service area for Example Cleaning Co. is Example County only. What general city or neighborhood are you asking about? An owner must confirm whether the address is covered.

**Draft 14 — option 2.** Thanks for asking about the cleaning policy. To avoid giving you an inaccurate answer about cancellations, refunds, specialty tasks or insurance, I will refer this to the business owner for a verified response.
