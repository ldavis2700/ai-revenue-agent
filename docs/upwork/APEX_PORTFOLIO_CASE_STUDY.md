**Project description:**

I designed and directed APEX, an independent AI-assisted automation project for
finding, evaluating, and managing legitimate revenue opportunities through an
evidence-backed job-to-cash workflow.

The system normalizes and deduplicates opportunities, screens prohibited or
high-risk work, ranks candidates by buyer intent, expected value, execution
confidence, time to cash, payment risk, and recurring value, and preserves
consent and suppression safeguards.

I also established the QA and transaction-integrity requirements. APEX cannot
claim that a proposal was submitted, a buyer replied, a contract was accepted,
work passed QA, delivery occurred, an invoice was issued, or revenue was
collected without matching provider evidence. State changes are atomic,
idempotent, and covered by automated regression tests. Verified revenue
reporting separates gross receipts, provider fees, refunds, and net cash.

This is an independent portfolio project built with AI-assisted development and
automated validation. It is not presented as client work, and no client revenue
or performance result is claimed.
