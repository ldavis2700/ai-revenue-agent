RMC Family Enterprises LLC

Find where consented home-service leads are falling through.

This proposed service maps missed calls, slow responses, inconsistent qualification, routing gaps, and booking friction. A bounded pilot automates one approved path and measures results against the buyer’s own source of truth.

No booking, revenue, savings, or ROI is guaranteed. Automation proceeds only with authorized integrations, documented consent and opt-out controls, human escalation, and measurable acceptance criteria.

Who may be a fit

  • U.S. HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, landscaping, cleaning, pest-control, and similar appointment-driven businesses
  • At least 20 consented inbound leads or calls per month
  • A measurable missed-call, after-hours, response-time, or follow-up gap
  • An authorized CRM, calendar, scheduler, or other source of truth
  • A documented opt-out process, suppression list, quiet hours, and human escalation owner

Bought or scraped lists, emergency dispatch, missing consent controls, unavailable authorized integrations, and requests for guaranteed outcomes are not eligible.

Proposed offer ladder

1. Paid diagnostic

$250–$500 fixed price test band

Document the current workflow, establish redacted baseline evidence, identify leakage and risks, and define one measurable pilot.

2. Bounded pilot

$1,500–$3,000 fixed price test band

One consented lead source, one qualification/routing path, one authorized booking destination, and one human escalation queue.

3. Managed automation

$500–$1,500 monthly test band

Integration monitoring, exception handling, QA sampling, consent-control checks, workflow tuning, and an auditable monthly report.

What the pilot must prove

  • No contact to suppressed or opted-out records
  • No duplicate outbound action
  • Auditable routing, escalation, and reconciliation lineage
  • Agreed response-time and misroute thresholds
  • Booked and attended outcomes reconciled to the buyer’s source of truth
  • Measured delivery, API/inference, and required human-time costs
  • Tested rollback and manual override

Outcome-pricing gate

Usage or outcome pricing is not offered until a paid pilot establishes reliable attribution. Any later outcome component requires an objective billable event, source of truth, attribution window, exclusions, fee, monthly cap, dispute process, and human escalation. Messages, leads, or bookings are never represented as collected client revenue.
