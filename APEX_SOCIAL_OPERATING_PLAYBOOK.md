## What the current official sources establish

- YouTube early YPP access in eligible regions starts at 500 subscribers, three public uploads in 90 days, and either 3,000 qualifying long-form watch hours in 365 days or 3 million qualifying Shorts views in 90 days. Advertising access uses 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 qualifying watch hours or 10 million qualifying Shorts views. Other policy, region, review and feature requirements still apply. Shorts-feed watch time is not long-form watch time. Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/94522 and https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851
- YouTube excludes repetitive/mass-produced inauthentic content from monetization. Original production and meaningful commentary matter; a new caption or voiceover alone is not an originality guarantee. Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392
- YouTube requires disclosure of realistic synthetic or meaningfully altered content in covered cases. Disclosure alone does not make otherwise ineligible content monetizable. Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491
- Facebook Content Monetization is described as invitation-based, with an interest form in the Professional Dashboard. Meta emphasizes original work; superficial edits to others' work can be deprioritized. Sources: https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/creator-fast-track-grow-your-audience-earn-money-on-facebook/ and https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/rewarding-original-creators-on-facebook/
- Instagram Gifts eligibility must be checked in the account's Professional Dashboard. An older official announcement is not a current universal follower threshold. Source: https://about.fb.com/news/2023/02/earn-money-on-instagram-with-gifts/
- TikTok's official overview describes Creator Rewards as supporting original videos at least one minute long. Its detailed eligibility page did not return readable text during this review, so exact current follower/view/account-type thresholds are deliberately not encoded here. Verify in TikTok Studio and the official page before acting. Sources: https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/tiktok-creator-fund-us/who-is-eligible-us and https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-rewards-program/creator-rewards-program
- LinkedIn offers free Service Pages, with some premium-only features. Choose profile versus company ownership carefully. Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a569535
- Pinterest does not itself arrange payment for affiliate/brand partnerships. Use original useful Pins and disclose commercial relationships. Sources: https://create.pinterest.com/creators/grow-and-succeed/ and https://policy.pinterest.com/en/commercial-and-branded-content-guidelines

## Competency curriculum and evidence of mastery

Maintain separate states for each platform and each competency: researched, prepared, demonstrated, monetized, repeatably_profitable. A written playbook earns researched/prepared status only.

| Competency | Required demonstration |
| --- | --- |
| Platform selection | Document audience, content fit, current rules, usable access, costs and a feasible revenue path |
| Account creation and security | Verify authentic ownership, correct account type, working recovery, appropriate 2FA and scoped operator access |
| Positioning and profile | Publish accurate name, description, visual identity and a working relevant destination; preserve brand boundaries |
| Research and originality | Produce an original brief backed by credible sources and viewer needs; maintain asset-rights records |
| Production | Deliver a coherent script, clear audio, readable captions, correct export and verified claims; inspect the finished result |
| Packaging | Create truthful titles/thumbnails/hooks; compare results without misleading imagery or fabricated outcomes |
| Distribution | Verify the exact authorized account, provider-required approval, processing success, intended visibility and public URL |
| Community | Answer relevant permitted questions accurately, respect opt-outs and escalation rules, and record meaningful feedback |
| Conversion | Verify landing page, explicit contact consent, delivery scope and authorized collection path end to end |
| Native monetization | Confirm eligibility, acceptance and payout setup from the platform, not a follower-count guess |
| Optimization | Test one change at a time, record costs and comparable results, and retain supported lessons |
| Revenue accounting | Reconcile external settled receipts, fees, refunds and delivery costs without duplicate attribution |

Operational mastery requires three consecutive end-to-end successful publishing/measurement cycles on that platform with no unresolved policy or delivery failure.
Monetization mastery additionally requires at least two independently settled positive-contribution outcomes from different content cohorts or customers, and verified fulfillment.
These are APEX's internal evidence standards, not platform guarantees. Do not mark any platform mastered from this document.

## Account creation procedure

1. Inventory existing accounts through connected providers before registering anything. Record platform, public handle/URL, owner entity, account type and intended audience. Never buy aged accounts or create clones to evade enforcement.
2. Prefer existing RMC AI Workshop for APEX tutorials. Keep HAEVEN, RMC CLASSICS, celebrity commentary and child-directed content separate by purpose.
3. Check handle availability through the provider. Prepare a consistent name, bio, avatar/banner and public support destination; do not claim a handle is reserved until registration is confirmed.
4. Use the owner's verified business details through the official sign-up flow. Never invent age, address, company verification or identity.
5. Complete authorized, delegable setup. Stop only the affected step for actual account-holder OAuth consent, OTP/phone verification, CAPTCHA, identity/tax attestation or required signatures. Keep independent production/research work moving.
6. Configure recovery and 2FA through secure provider flows. Do not place passwords, tokens, recovery codes, tax records or bank details in repository files or prompts.
7. Grant only necessary publishing/analytics access. An account connected for analytics may not be able to publish; a publishing connection may not have private analytics.
8. Record a verified account URL and actual permissions. Separately track monetization eligibility, enrollment, payout verification and limitations.
9. Do not purchase subscriptions or enroll paid services merely to complete setup. Financial changes retain the existing authorization boundaries.

## Content production and distribution procedure

Use a brief containing audience problem, promised useful outcome, original angle, factual sources, demonstration plan, call to action, commercial disclosures and asset rights.
Show real demonstrations with synthetic/sample customer data. Never expose private records or invent client results.

Produce one complete, useful YouTube tutorial, then derive genuinely useful Shorts, a visual checklist and a platform-specific explanation when appropriate. Adapt the framing and formatting for each destination instead of flooding every account with identical promotional text.
For faceless production use original screen recordings, diagrams, properly licensed footage and narration with appropriate rights. No fake celebrity endorsements or realistic fabricated news footage.
For celebrity commentary distinguish documented facts, allegations and analysis; retain sources and licensed assets. For children's content verify current audience designation, privacy and platform rules before launch, and do not use adult sales tactics aimed at children.

Check every asset: rights, factual accuracy, captions, audio, aspect ratio, safe areas, disclosure, destination link, data privacy and viewer value. Platform-cleared music on one network is not automatically licensed for another.

Before publishing, resolve the exact account, media hash, title/caption, thumbnail, disclosures, audience setting, visibility and timing. Honor the connected provider's exact-post approval requirement. Prepared or approved content is not published content.
Use a deduplication key based on platform + account + asset hash + intended slot. On a timeout, check status before retrying.
After publishing, verify provider receipt, processing state, intended visibility and URL. Diagnose failed posts without duplicating successful destinations.

## Monetization and measurement

Near-term: use useful demonstrations to generate consented inquiries for the existing bounded AI customer-response service. The repository lists a $100 setup and optional $99/month managed offer; validate scope, delivery capacity and costs before representing availability. Never promise autonomous delivery beyond implemented capabilities.

Additional paths: approved affiliate offers with clear disclosures; truthful sponsorships supported by real audience data; useful digital products with support/refund scope; memberships with deliverable capacity; native platform payments after actual approval.
Do not wait for ad thresholds to test legitimate service demand, but do not promote a broken checkout or unsupported payment method. Preserve the Stripe pause and verify an existing authorized alternative.

Record per asset: platform/account, content ID, URL, publication time, topic, source/rights evidence, production cost, impressions, views, watch time, retention, clicks, consented inquiries, accepted paid work, settled gross, fees, refunds, delivery cost and contribution.
Use consistent time windows and denominators. Unknown is not zero. Platform estimated earnings, attributed conversions, collected cash and bank receipts are separate states.
Reconcile each payment once across cross-posts; report assisted conversions separately. Never sum the same receipt across YouTube, Facebook and Instagram.
Contribution = settled revenue minus refunds, processor/platform fees and directly attributable production/delivery costs. Show shared overhead separately. Exclude pass-through taxes from earned revenue.

For initial content experiments, record a seven-day and 28-day review where data is available. These are observation windows, not promises of profitability. Compare similar formats/topics and change one major variable at a time.
Scale only when output quality, fulfillment, permission and cost capacity support it. Retire a weak experiment only after recording adequate exposure or the reason exposure could not be obtained. Do not buy engagement or manufacture comments.
